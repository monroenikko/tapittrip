# Stays and Luggage-Aware Arrival Transport

- Milestone: `M2b`
- Status: `implemented` (Backend foundation + Flutter mobile UI + Waypoint guidance)
- Owner: `engineering`
- Last verified: `2026-11-20`

## Purpose and traveler outcome

Empowers travelers to manage already-booked accommodations (hotels, hostels, rentals, staying with friends/family) across sequential cities without booking through the app.

Provides luggage-aware, explainable transport comparisons from arrival airports/stations to the saved stay, scoring options based on luggage burden (backpacks, carry-ons, checked bags, oversized bags, and strollers) while providing honest fallback navigation handoffs when verified transit data is unavailable.

## How it works

### Stay ideas when no accommodation is saved (2026-11-10)

- The empty Stays tab retains **Add Accommodation** and shows **Ideas for where to stay**. Opening it automatically calls `POST /api/v1/trips/{trip}/stay-recommendations` for suitable areas and lodging to research, using saved itinerary stops/dates; without stops, the trip destination is the fallback. No detailed itinerary or purchase is required.
- AI guidance asks for reasons a base fits the route, including separate bases for multi-city trips, without assuming budget, party needs, prices, availability, travel times, or accessibility. Cards accept only hotel-category lodging suggestions. Ideas do not create a stay or a booking.
- Cards reuse **View place** and **Directions** map links. Suggestions are unverified; users confirm venue details themselves. Existing saved stays hide the section and prevent new requests. Itinerary edits refresh ideas after a 600 ms debounce while the section is mounted.
- Failed requests offer manual retry and keep manual accommodation entry available. Missing/unavailable Gemini shows explicitly labeled general guidance rather than fabricated hotel suggestions. Results are temporary screen state, not persisted offline or generated by a background job.
- Privacy/security: the dedicated endpoint checks trip membership and uses the existing Gemini adapter with minimized itinerary context. No database schema, new keys, or location permissions are required. External maps open only after a tap. English/Filipino copy follows the resolved app locale.
- Verification: `flutter test test/stay_recommendations_test.dart test/recommendations_test.dart` passed (13 tests); `flutter analyze` passed. Tests cover saved-stay suppression, itinerary changes, no-itinerary trips, lodging-only cards, fallback, failure/retry, and 320 px/200% text layout. Live Gemini and device map handoff remain manual checks. 1. **Independent Stays CRUD**:
   - Supports multiple sequential or overlapping stays per trip in different cities.
   - Travelers can save stay name, address, coordinates, check-in and check-out times in the stay's local timezone, phone number, and private notes.
   - **Privacy & Encryption**: Sensitive `booking_reference` values are encrypted at rest using Laravel's `'encrypted'` casts. They are never exposed in public previews or push notification payloads.
2. **Non-Destructive Itinerary Integration**:
   - Stays can optionally generate linked `Check-in: {name}` and `Check-out: {name}` itinerary items.
   - Linked items reference `stay_id` with `nullOnDelete`. Deleting or modifying a stay never orphans or destroys the user's itinerary activities.
3. **Luggage-Aware Transport Comparisons & Scoring**:
   - Travelers configure their luggage profile via presets (`light`, `standard`, `heavy`, `custom`) or explicit item counts (`backpacks`, `carry_ons`, `checked_bags`, `oversized`, `stroller`).
   - The comparison engine evaluates arrival options against luggage burden and party size:
     - **Private Transfer / Taxi**: 0 transfers, door-to-door, highest luggage capacity (fits 4+ bags and strollers). Ranked **Most Convenient** when heavy luggage or strollers are present. Economical for larger groups due to flat vehicle pricing.
     - **Airport Express Rail**: Direct or 1 transfer, dedicated luggage racks, elevator-accessible terminals. Ranked **Fastest** and **Most Convenient** for light or standard baggage profiles.
     - **Public City Transit**: Lowest overall cost for solo or light travelers, but incurs heavy penalties for transfers and walking distance when transporting heavy baggage. Ranked **Lowest Estimated Cost**.
4. **Zero-Typing Arrival Transport Suggestions**:
   - When opening the Arrival Transport sheet, the app automatically pre-fills the arrival origin using the detected city airport or primary transit terminal (`nearestAirportName` + `nearestAirportCode`, or `stationName`).
   - Travelers immediately see ranked route options without manually searching or typing terminal names.
5. **End-to-End Waypoint Guidance & Dedicated Last-Mile Walk Navigation**:
   - Each route option explicitly surfaces the Boarding Point (`boarding_location`, e.g. Airport Taxi Stand or Express Rail Platform), Drop-off Station (`dropoff_location`, e.g. Shinjuku Station South Exit or Hong Kong Station Exit A), Drop-off Address (`dropoff_address`), and last-mile walking distance/time (`last_mile_walk_meters`, `last_mile_walk_minutes`).
   - Dedicated station-to-stay walking directions URL (`last_mile_directions_url`) specifies origin as the drop-off station, destination as the stay, and `travelmode=walking`, launched via a one-tap **Walk Map** action.
6. **Honest Limitations & Maps Handoff**:
   - If an origin lacks verified transit schedules or coordinates, the system returns `verified: false` with status `unverified` and immediately provides a direct Google Maps directions URL (`google_maps_directions_url`), ensuring no fabricated transit times, fares, or accessibility claims.

## Stay recommendations API

- `POST /api/v1/trips/{trip}/stay-recommendations`, authenticated with Sanctum; no request body. Accepted viewers/editors/owners can request advisory results. Rate limit: 6 requests/minute/user, with `429 rate_limited` and `Retry-After`.
- `GenerateStayRecommendationsService` owns the use case under `app/Services/Stays`. `StayRecommendationProvider` is the injectable boundary, implemented by `GeminiStayRecommendationProvider` using the existing Gemini adapter. The server owns the prompt; Flutter no longer sends it through the generic assistant endpoint.
- Returns `data` with `reply`, `category`, `context_summary`, and `suggested_actions`. `context_summary.status` is `ready`, `unavailable`, or `stay_exists`; `verified` is always false. Existing stays are checked before and after provider generation. `unavailable` is a successful fallback response with no invented lodging cards.
- The backend accepts at most three lodging cards, deduplicates by normalized place/title, bounds string lengths, strips HTML, and discards unknown payload fields/URLs. The `hotel` category covers lodging ideas, including hostels/rentals; it does not save a stay type. Nothing is booked or persisted. Responses include `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- Error contract: `401 unauthenticated`, `403 forbidden`, `404 not_found`, `429 rate_limited`, `500 internal_error`. No request fields exist to validate; saved stays use a successful no-op rather than conflict. See [OpenAPI](../api/openapi.yaml) for full schemas and a synthetic example.
- Rollout: deploy this backend before the updated Flutter client. The generic assistant endpoint remains compatible. No migration or new environment variable; existing `GEMINI_API_KEY`/model configuration applies. Results remain transient; AI/device checks are not performed by automated tests.

Example request (local placeholders only):

```http
POST /api/v1/trips/{trip_uuid}/stay-recommendations
Authorization: Bearer <token>
Accept: application/json
```

## Architecture and data flow

- **Data Models**:
  - `stays`: UUID, `trip_id` (FK `trips`), `place_id` (FK `places`, nullable), `name`, `stay_type` (`hotel`, `hostel`, `rental`, `friends_family`), `address`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `check_in_at`, `check_out_at`, `timezone`, `phone`, `booking_reference` (encrypted), `notes`, `luggage_profile` (JSON), `version`, timestamps, soft deletes.
  - `itinerary_items`: augmented with nullable `stay_id` foreign key (`onDelete('set null')`).
- **Services** (`app/Services/Stays`):
  - `CreateStayService`: Validates check-in < check-out, creates stay, optionally links/creates place, and provisions check-in/out itinerary items.
  - `UpdateStayService`: Updates stay attributes, increments version, and updates linked itinerary item titles and timestamps.
  - `DeleteStayService`: Decouples linked itinerary items without orphaning and soft deletes stay.
  - `CompareStayTransportService`: Deterministically evaluates transit options against luggage burden, party size, and provider attribution.
- **Security & Authorization**: All stay endpoints enforce `TripPolicy` (`view` for reading and transport comparisons, `update` for creating, editing, and deleting).
